Safeguarding questions

How does the Center work to protect children?

The Center is committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, neglect, and discrimination. Everyone representing the Center should put children's best interests first, listen to them, respect their boundaries, follow staff guidance, and raise concerns promptly. Visitors should arrange visits in advance and must not seek unsupervised contact with a child.

What safeguarding standards should staff, volunteers, and visitors follow?

Our stated commitment is to treat every child with dignity, respect their voice and privacy, act in their best interests, and prevent harm. Visitors and other representatives should follow staff instructions, remain in approved areas, respect each child's boundaries, and never arrange unsupervised contact. Please ask the Center directly about its current formal policies, training, and staff or volunteer screening procedures.

How can I report a safeguarding concern?

If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. To report a concern to the Center, use the reporting form below. Share only what is needed to explain what happened; do not investigate it yourself. Can I take or share photographs, videos, or stories about children?

Do not photograph, film, record, or publish identifying details or personal stories about a child without prior permission and appropriate informed consent. Consent does not override a safeguarding review or staff instruction. Never share a child's name, location, or other details that could identify them.

Will my report or enquiry be kept confidential?

Please share only the information needed to explain your concern. The website does not submit or store the report form; it opens an email draft that you choose whether to send. Once sent, the Center receives the information in your email and may need to use it to respond or take follow-up action. Ask the Center how it handles and retains reports.

Child protection commitment

Community Progressive Focus Center is committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, neglect, and discrimination. Everyone representing the Center should act in children's best interests, respect their voices and boundaries, and raise concerns promptly.

  • Follow staff instructions and remain in approved areas.
  • Never arrange unsupervised contact with a child.
  • Respect each child's voice, privacy, and boundaries.
  • Raise concerns promptly through the reporting option below.

Visiting hours & arrangements

Please contact the Center in advance to confirm a visit. Do not visit residential or programme spaces without permission. The current enquiry hours listed by the Center are:

  • Monday to Friday: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm
  • Weekends and public holidays: contact the Center to confirm availability
  • All visits: subject to confirmation and staff guidance

These are enquiry hours, not confirmation of drop-in access. Visits must be arranged in advance.

Photography rules

A small reminder: protect every child's privacy.
  • Do not photograph, film, or record children without prior permission and appropriate informed consent.
  • Consent does not replace a safeguarding review; follow staff directions, including no-photography rules.
  • Never post identifying details, names, personal stories, or location tags.

Privacy notice for enquiries

When you use an enquiry form on this website, your browser opens your default email application with the details you entered. The website itself does not submit or store the form contents. You can review and edit the email before sending it.

If you send an email, the Center receives the information you choose to provide and may use it to respond and take necessary follow-up action. Please do not include sensitive personal information about a child unless it is necessary to raise a safeguarding concern.

The Center's specific retention period, data-protection contact, and procedures for access or deletion requests have not been provided. Please contact cpfcenter@gmail.com with privacy questions and ask the Center to confirm its approved retention policy.

Privacy information should be reviewed and updated by the Center to reflect its approved policies and applicable law.
