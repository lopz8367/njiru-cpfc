Donate items

Share useful, needed essentials.

Please check current needs with the Center before purchasing or dropping off items. Suitable needs may include:

  • Notebooks, pens, and learning materials
  • Age-appropriate books and learning resources
  • Sports and recreation equipment
  • Health, hygiene, and everyday essentials
Arrange drop-off first

Drop-off times and accepted items must be confirmed in advance. The Center is located in Njiru, behind ACK Church. Email cpfcenter@gmail.com for directions and safe delivery arrangements.
